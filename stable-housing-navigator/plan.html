🔄 Backup Plans

If the current plan doesn't work, here's what to try next.

If rental assistance is denied or out of funding

Try: Catholic Community Services (206-323-6336), Salvation Army (211), other 211-listed programs.

If eviction proceeds despite assistance

Call Northwest Justice Project (1-888-201-1014) immediately for eviction defense representation.

If you must leave your current housing

Call 211 for emergency shelter. Contact Coordinated Entry for housing programs. McKinney-Vento protects your children's school enrollment.
