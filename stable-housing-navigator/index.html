What this navigator does

Built to guide you through every step

🎯

Smart Triage

Calm, plain-language questions identify your most urgent needs first and sort you into the right support pathway — no wrong door.

🔍

Barrier Detection

Every barrier has a workaround. We identify eviction history, credit issues, voucher problems, disability needs, and more — then show you the path past them.

📋

Your Stability Plan

A personalized plan with today's top 3 actions, upcoming deadlines, documents needed, backup options, and who to call.

⚖️

Rights & Protections

When your situation triggers a legal protection — eviction rules, fair housing, disability accommodation, DV rights — we explain it clearly and tell you what to do.

✉️

Letters & Scripts

Ready-to-use letters for landlord accommodation requests, rental assistance, eviction responses, utility shutoff prevention, and more.

📁

Document Vault

Organize your lease, notices, IDs, income proof, and more. Know what you have and what's missing. Never lose a critical document.

👨‍👩‍👧

Family Module

Special support for families — school stability rights, childcare, child-safe housing needs, and keeping children connected to services.

🔄

Follow-Through System

Track applications, caseworker calls, deadlines, and next steps. Never lose track of where things stand. Always know what to do if something doesn't work.

🔒

Private & Secure

Everything stays on your device. Nothing is sent to a server. Sensitive sections have privacy warnings. DV safety features built in.
